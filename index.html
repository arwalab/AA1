4-Week Ladder
Progression
Same workouts, rising demand. Light weights only build mass if you keep raising the challenge — this is how you do it without heavier dumbbells.
WK1
Groove the Form
Hit lower rep targets. Leave 2–3 reps in the tank (RPE 7). Master technique + 3-sec lowering on every rep.
WK2
Add Reps
Push to top of each rep range. Cut rest to 60s. Add +1 set to the first exercise. RPE 7–8.
WK3
Add Intensity
Slow lowering to 4 sec. Drop set on last set of each lift. Train at RPE 8 throughout.
WK4
Peak Week
Superset lower-body + pull moves. Rest 45s. Top of all rep ranges. Push hard — keep as new baseline.
Whoop Integration
🟢
Green (67–100%)
Train as programmed. Full intensity, push to the top of all rep ranges.
🟡
Yellow (34–66%)
Train but drop one set per exercise. Don't push to failure. Alo Flow is ideal here.
🔴
Red (0–33%)
Swap session for Alo yoga or a walk. Training on red wastes the surplus — recovery wins.

Staple Kitchen
Recipes
8 staple recipes that cover both plans. Tap any card to expand ingredients and steps.
🍗
Batch Garlic Lemon Chicken
~165 kcal/100g31g protein/100g30 min
Prep once, eat all week. Both plans. Grill 1.5kg — covers 5 days.
Ingredients (1.5 kg chicken)
  • 1.5 kg chicken breasts
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 lemons, juiced + zested
  • 1 tsp cumin, 1 tsp paprika
  • Salt + pepper to taste
Method
Pound chicken breasts to even thickness (~2cm) so they cook uniformly.
Mix olive oil, garlic, lemon juice/zest, cumin, paprika, salt. Marinate 15 min (or overnight).
Grill on medium-high 6–7 min per side, or bake at 200°C for 22–25 min.
Rest 5 min before slicing. Portion into 180g (him) and 150g (her) containers.
🐟
Pan-Seared Salmon with Tahini
~280 kcal/fillet34g protein12 min
Her bone-density dinner. Omega-3 + calcium from tahini drizzle.
Ingredients (2 fillets)
  • 2 salmon fillets (150g each)
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt, pepper, cumin
  • 2 tbsp tahini
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • 1 garlic clove, minced
  • 2 tbsp warm water
Method
Pat salmon dry. Season with salt, pepper, pinch of cumin.
Heat olive oil in pan over medium-high. Place salmon skin-side down. Cook 4 min without moving.
Flip carefully. Cook 3 min more until just flaking.
Mix tahini, lemon juice, garlic, warm water until smooth and pourable. Drizzle over salmon and serve.

Overnight Oats (His Breakfast)
~420 kcal22g protein5 min prep
Prep 5 jars on Friday. Grab-and-go every workday morning.
Ingredients (1 jar)
  • 80g rolled oats
  • 200ml whole milk
  • 1 tbsp chia seeds
  • 80g mixed berries (frozen OK)
  • 1 tsp honey
  • Optional: 1 scoop whey protein (stir in)
Method
Add oats, chia seeds to a jar or container with lid.
Pour milk over. Stir well. Add berries and honey on top.
Seal and refrigerate overnight (minimum 4 hrs). Lasts 3 days in fridge.
Stir before eating. Add whey protein if needed for extra protein hit.

High-Protein Labneh Bowl
~320 kcal20g protein5 min
Her calcium cornerstone. Works as breakfast, snack, or side.
Ingredients
  • 150g full-fat labneh
  • 2 tbsp tahini
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • Handful cherry tomatoes, halved
  • Cucumber, sliced
  • Pinch za'atar or dried thyme
  • 2 slices sourdough or pita
Method
Spread labneh in a wide bowl. Make a well in the center.
Drizzle olive oil + tahini into the well.
Scatter tomatoes and cucumber around the edges. Finish with za'atar.
Serve with bread for dipping. Eat immediately.

Roasted Sweet Potato + Veg Tray
~180 kcal/serve4g protein45 min
Batch on Friday. Covers both plans for 4 evenings. Reheat in 2 min.
Ingredients (4 portions)
  • 4 medium sweet potatoes
  • 400g broccoli florets
  • 300g zucchini, sliced
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp cumin, 1 tsp garlic powder
  • Salt + pepper
Method
Preheat oven to 200°C. Place whole sweet potatoes on one tray — pierce with fork.
Toss broccoli and zucchini in olive oil + spices. Spread on second tray.
Roast veg 20–25 min. Sweet potatoes 40–45 min until soft through.
Cool, portion into 4 containers. Refrigerate. Reheat at mealtime.

Lean Beef with Garlic Spinach
~380 kcal42g protein15 min
Iron + creatine source. Shared dinner — both plans. 3–4×/week ideal for her bone goals.
Ingredients (2 serves)
  • 300g lean beef fillet or rump, sliced
  • 200g baby spinach
  • 4 garlic cloves, sliced thin
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • ½ lemon, juiced
  • Salt, pepper, chilli flakes
Method
Season beef with salt + pepper. Heat 1 tbsp oil in a pan over high heat.
Sear beef 2–3 min per side (medium-rare to medium). Rest 5 min, then slice.
In same pan, add remaining oil + garlic. Sauté 1 min until golden.
Add spinach, toss until wilted (2 min). Finish with lemon juice + chilli. Serve with beef on top.
🧆
Spiced Lentil Soup
~280 kcal/bowl16g protein25 min
Plant protein + iron + folate. Her rest day lunch or his light dinner option.
Ingredients (4 bowls)
  • 300g red lentils, rinsed
  • 1 large onion, diced
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tsp cumin, 1 tsp turmeric, ½ tsp coriander
  • 1.2L vegetable broth or water
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt, lemon to serve
Method
Sauté onion in olive oil 5 min until soft. Add garlic + spices, cook 1 min.
Add lentils + broth. Bring to boil, reduce heat. Simmer 20 min until lentils are completely soft.
Blend half the soup with a stick blender for a thick-but-textured finish.
Season generously. Serve with lemon squeeze + drizzle of olive oil + toasted bread.

High-Cal Desk Shake (Her)
~380 kcal30g protein2 min
For days when desk snacks aren't enough. No blender needed.
Ingredients
  • 250ml whole milk
  • 1 scoop whey protein (vanilla or unflavored)
  • 1 tbsp almond butter
  • 1 tbsp tahini
  • 1 tsp honey
  • Optional: 3g creatine (stir in)
Method
Add all ingredients to a shaker bottle or jar with lid.
Shake or stir vigorously for 30 seconds until combined. Nut butter may need extra mixing.
Drink immediately. This is your emergency calorie hit on days you miss a meal alarm.

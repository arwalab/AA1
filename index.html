Recipes
🍗
Batch Garlic Lemon Chicken
165 kcal/100g31g protein30 min
Both plans. Grill 1.5kg — covers 5 days.
Ingredients
  • 1.5kg chicken breasts
  • 3 tbsp olive oil, 4 garlic cloves
  • 2 lemons juiced + zested
  • 1 tsp cumin, 1 tsp paprika, salt + pepper
Method
Pound breasts to even thickness (~2cm).
Mix oil, garlic, lemon, spices. Marinate 15 min.
Grill 6–7 min/side or bake 200°C for 25 min.
Rest 5 min. Portion 180g (him) / 150g (her).
🐟
Pan-Seared Salmon + Tahini
280 kcal34g protein12 min
Her bone-density dinner. Omega-3 + calcium.
Ingredients (2 fillets)
  • 2 salmon fillets (150g each)
  • 1 tbsp olive oil, salt, pepper, cumin
  • 2 tbsp tahini, 1 lemon, 1 garlic clove, 2 tbsp warm water
Method
Pat salmon dry. Season with salt, pepper, cumin.
Heat oil, skin-side down, 4 min. Flip, 3 min more.
Mix tahini + lemon + garlic + water. Drizzle and serve.
🥣
Overnight Oats (Ahmed)
420 kcal22g protein5 min prep
Prep 5 jars Friday. Grab-and-go every morning.
Ingredients (1 jar)
  • 80g rolled oats, 200ml whole milk
  • 1 tbsp chia seeds, 80g berries, 1 tsp honey
Method
Add oats + chia to jar. Pour milk over, stir.
Add berries + honey. Seal, refrigerate overnight.
Stir before eating. Lasts 3 days in fridge.
🥩
Lean Beef + Garlic Spinach
380 kcal42g protein15 min
Iron + creatine. Shared dinner. 3–4×/week.
Ingredients (2 serves)
  • 300g lean beef fillet, 200g baby spinach
  • 4 garlic cloves, 2 tbsp olive oil, ½ lemon
Method
Season beef. Sear in hot oil 2–3 min/side. Rest + slice.
In same pan, sauté garlic 1 min. Add spinach, toss until wilted.
Finish with lemon juice. Serve spinach under beef.
